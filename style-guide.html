Design System Spec

HoneyChain Style Guide

Designed specifically for rural beekeepers with low digital literacy. Focused on extreme clarity, high contrast, zero cognitive clutter, and generous touch targets.

1. Color Palette (Strict 3-Tone)

No blue, green, red, or multi-accent palettes. Every element uses milky white, warm orange, or dark charcoal brown.

Milky White (#FDFBF7)
Primary background for all screens
Warm Orange (#E8892B)
Single accent color for primary buttons & highlights
Dark Charcoal Brown (#3A2E26)
High-readability text & icon color

2. Typography Scale

One simple, large sans-serif font (Plus Jakarta Sans). Minimum 18px body text, 28px+ headings, with generous line spacing.

Heading 1 (36px, Line-height 1.35)
Record Today’s Honey
Heading 2 (28px, Line-height 1.35)
Select Hive Box Number
Body Standard (20px, Line-height 1.65)

Please enter the total weight of the honey harvested from this hive today.

Caption / Helper (18px Minimum)

Tap the large orange button below when you are finished.

3. Iconography (Line Icons + Text)

Line icons are used sparingly and always paired with text labels. Never display icon-only buttons or ambiguous symbols.

Morning Harvest
Estimated Payment: ₹4,200
Batch Saved to HoneyChain
